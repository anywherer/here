Who are you?

Your personality changes dramatically throughout your life.

Your beliefs and values grow and shift every day.

All the atoms in your body are replaced every seven years.

The Ship of Theseus asks whether or not a ship that has had all of its components replaced one by one would remain the same ship.

Who are you?

As I always like to say, you are the sum of your experiences.

Not your human body

Not your personality

Not your status

Not your belongings

Not your beliefs and values.

At your core, you are the sum of your experiences.

Life: the sequence of physical and mental experiences that make up the existence of an individual.
