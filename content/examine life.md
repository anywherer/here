> "The unexamined life is not worth living."

Socrates believed that a life lived without critical reflection, without questioning one's beliefs, values, and actions, was a life lived in ignorance and therefore lacked true value or purpose.

Whet is the unexamined life?

Since you are the only conscious being responsible for yourself, the weight of introspection falls on you alone.
